# 🦇 1. BATMAN – THE DARK KNIGHT

> "It's not who I am underneath, but what I do that defines me."

**Real Name:** Bruce Wayne  
**City:** Gotham City  
**First Appearance:** Detective Comics #27 (1939)  
**Special Abilities:** Intelligence, martial arts, detective skills.

### Origin Story
As a child, Bruce Wayne witnessed the tragic murder of his parents. This incident changed his life forever. He dedicated himself to fighting crime and protecting innocent people.

### Superpowers & Gadgets
- No supernatural powers.
- Exceptional detective and strategic thinking skills.
- Uses advanced technology, the Batmobile, and Batarangs.
- Highly skilled in martial arts and hand-to-hand combat.

### What Makes Him Unique?
Batman proves that you don't need superpowers to become a superhero. His greatest weapons are his intelligence, determination, and courage.
