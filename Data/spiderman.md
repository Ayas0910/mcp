# 🕷️ 3. SPIDER-MAN – YOUR FRIENDLY NEIGHBORHOOD HERO

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

**Real Name:** Peter Parker  
**City:** New York City  
**First Appearance:** Amazing Fantasy #15 (1962)  
**Special Abilities:** Spider-sense, wall crawling, enhanced strength.

### Origin Story
Peter Parker was an ordinary teenager who was bitten by a radioactive spider. The bite gave him extraordinary spider-like abilities. After experiencing a personal tragedy involving his Uncle Ben, Peter understood that having great power also means having great responsibility.

### Superpowers & Abilities
- Can climb walls and ceilings.
- Possesses an extraordinary spider-sense.
- Enhanced strength, agility, and reflexes.
- Uses web-shooters to swing between buildings.
- Excellent scientific and engineering knowledge.

### What Makes Him Unique?
Unlike many superheroes, Peter struggles with everyday problems such as studies, relationships, money, and family responsibilities. His humanity makes him relatable to millions of people.
