# 🦸 2. SUPERMAN – THE MAN OF STEEL

> "Dreams save us. Dreams lift us up and transform us."

**Real Name:** Clark Kent (Kal-El)  
**Home Planet:** Krypton  
**City:** Metropolis  
**First Appearance:** Action Comics #1 (1938)  
**Special Abilities:** Super strength, flight, heat vision, super speed.

### Origin Story
Superman was born on the planet Krypton. Before his planet was destroyed, his parents sent him to Earth in a spaceship. He was raised by the Kent family in Smallville and eventually became Earth's greatest protector.

### Superpowers & Abilities
- Incredible physical strength.
- Ability to fly at tremendous speeds.
- Heat vision and freezing breath.
- X-ray vision and super hearing.
- Enhanced durability and super speed.

### What Makes Him Unique?
Superman represents hope, kindness, and justice. Despite having extraordinary powers, he believes in protecting humanity and doing what is right.
