# Revaluation Policy

## Revaluation Eligibility

Students who are dissatisfied with their semester examination results can apply for revaluation.

## Application Process

Students must submit their revaluation application through the university examination portal.

## Application Deadline

Revaluation applications must be submitted within 10 days from the date of result publication.

## Revaluation Fee

A revaluation fee of Rs. 600 per subject is applicable.

## Results

Revaluation results are published after the evaluation process is completed.

## Important Notes

Internal assessment marks are not included in the revaluation process.
