# Internal Assessment Policy

## Assessment Components

Internal assessment consists of:
- Written tests
- Assignments
- Attendance and participation
- Practical assessments, where applicable

## Internal Marks

Internal assessment carries 40 marks, while the semester examination carries 60 marks.

## Internal Assessment Calculation

Internal marks are calculated based on performance in assessments conducted during the semester.

## Improvement

Students should contact their respective subject faculty if they believe their internal marks have been calculated incorrectly.

Internal marks cannot be changed without proper verification and approval.
