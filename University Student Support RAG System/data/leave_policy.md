# Student Leave Policy

## Medical Leave

Students who are unable to attend classes due to medical reasons should submit a medical certificate to their department.

## Leave Application

Students must inform their department about planned leave in advance whenever possible.

## Leave Approval

Leave applications must be approved by the department.

## Attendance Impact

Taking leave does not automatically exempt students from attendance requirements.

Medical certificates may be considered when applying for attendance condonation.

## Important Notes

Submitting a medical certificate does not guarantee approval of attendance condonation.
