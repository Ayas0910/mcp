# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% to be eligible to attend semester examinations.

## Shortage of Attendance

Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation, subject to university approval.

Students with attendance below 65% are generally not eligible to appear for semester examinations.

## Attendance Calculation

Attendance is calculated based on the total number of working days conducted during the semester.

Approved medical leave does not automatically guarantee attendance exemption.

## Important Notes

- Attendance is monitored throughout the semester.
- Students should regularly check their attendance records.
- Any discrepancy should be reported to the department.
