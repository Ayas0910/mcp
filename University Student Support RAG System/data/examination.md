# Examination Policy

## Semester Examination

Semester examinations are conducted at the end of each semester.

Students must satisfy the minimum attendance requirement to be eligible for examinations.

## Examination Registration

Students must complete examination registration before the university deadline.

Late registration may be subject to additional fees and university approval.

## Passing Criteria

Students must secure the minimum passing marks prescribed for each subject.

A student who fails a subject must register for the arrear examination.

## Arrear Examination

Students can clear failed subjects by appearing for subsequent arrear examinations.

The examination schedule is published by the university.
